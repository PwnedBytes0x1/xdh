# 🎨 Theming & Customization

`xdh` includes 7 developer palettes configured for high readability across low-color Termux terminals, high-DPI desktop terminals, and truecolor emulators.

---

## 🌈 Supported Palettes

1. **`tokyo_night`** *(Default)*: Clean deep indigo and neon pastel accents.
2. **`dracula`**: High-contrast purple, pink, and vibrant greens.
3. **`catppuccin`** *(Mocha)*: Warm soothing pastel palette.
4. **`monokai`**: Classic hacker vibrant contrast (yellow, green, magenta).
5. **`nord`**: Arctic, north-bluish clean aesthetic.
6. **`cyberpunk`**: High-voltage neon cyan, electric yellow, and pink on near-black.
7. **`gruvbox`**: Earthy retro warm colors with reduced eye strain.

---

## 🔄 Dynamic Switching

Switch themes immediately without restarting `xdh`:

```bash
# Using slash command:
/theme dracula

# Or cycling with keybindings:
Ctrl+T
```
